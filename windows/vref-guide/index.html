Set driver current (Vref)

Each A4988 has a small screw (trimpot) that sets how much current, and so how much torque, its motor gets. You measure a voltage on that screw (Vref) with your multimeter and turn it until it matches the target below.

Formula: current = Vref ÷ (8 × sense resistor). R100 = 0.1 Ω → Vref = current × 0.8.

3 · Do it
    Don't: plug or unplug a motor while 24 V is on (it can destroy the driver) · let the screwdriver touch two pins at once · go above 1.0 A on a driver without a fan blowing on it.
