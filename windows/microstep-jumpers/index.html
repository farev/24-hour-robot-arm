    No caps? Use bare wire
    1. Cut 4-5 cm from a spare jumper wire and strip all the insulation.
    3. Press it flat against the pin base. Nothing over 5 mm tall or past the 6 pins.
    4. Seat the driver and check it pushes fully down.

    A plugged-in jumper wire is 14 mm tall and the driver sits 8 mm above the pins, so it won't fit.

    Where to get caps (11)
    • California JOS 2.54 mm jumper caps, 100 pcs, $4.97 at Amazon, delivery Wed Nov 18 at the fastest (read 2026-11-16).
    • Free today: the Invention Studio desk or an ECE lab, or off old PC parts (motherboards, hard drives, CD drives).
    • Permanent: solder the bare wire to the pins. These settings don't change.
