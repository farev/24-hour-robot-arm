Start the motor bridge

  1. Flash firmware/motor_test to the Uno (Electronics panel).
  2. In Terminal, in the project folder: python3 tools/mpy.py tools/motor_bridge.py
  3. Pick the port above (/dev/cu.usbmodem…) and press Connect.
First run: Vref set, jumpers fitted, 24 V on, belt off.
