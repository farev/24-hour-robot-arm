## Blocking

1. **Gripper servo and horn fasteners**: the screws that hold the MG996R to the gripper base and crank gear A
   to the servo horn are not listed anywhere. The assembly has no part for them, and components.yaml has no
   entry, so nothing is bought or owned for them.
   - crank_gear.py:38-43 cuts 4 holes of Ø2.2 on a 7.0 mm circle (`HORN_SCREW_R = 7.0  # [est]`), with Ø3.8 × 2
     head counterbores in a 6.0 mm plate. That is an M2-class screw about 6-8 mm long. The user's stock is "M3
     and M4 socket head, 6-30 mm" (components.yaml:254), and M3 does not fit a Ø2.2 hole.
   - gripper_base.py:57-58 cuts 4 Ø2.6 pilots through the 5 mm plate for the servo tabs. Nothing records which
     screw goes in them. The servo's own tab screws are about 2.5 mm, and a Ø2.6 pilot gives them no grip. An M3
     from the user's stock would bite, but it is not in the screw count (components.yaml:259 lists 42 screws
     and none of them are for the servo).
   - The MG996R datasheet says only "comes with a selection of arms and hardware". The Miuzei listing
     (B0BZ4LVH4J) did not show what is in the box when I loaded it today.

   Fix: add 4 × M2 × 6-8 self-tapping screws to the cart (or confirm the Miuzei box has horn screws that fit Ø2.2
   with a head ≤ Ø3.6). Record 4 × M3 × 10 from the user's stock for the tab pilots, and check that an M3 passes
   the brass eyelets in the servo's rubber grommets.

## Should fix

1. **Hookup wire is recorded as owned, but the user did not say they have it.** components.yaml:355-367 renames
   the entry to "Jumper wires and hookup wire (your stock)" and has the 24 V buck input and the 6 V servo power
   running on "hookup wire (22 AWG or thicker)". The user's answer was "I have jumpers that I can use for 3 and 4"
   (questions/owned-hardware-wiring.yaml:57). Item (4) was "jumper wires or hookup wire", so the answer only
   covers jumpers.
   - Servo stall is 2.5 A at 6 V (MG996R datasheet, p.2). If chained Dupont jumpers (about 26-28 AWG) carry it
     over the ~73 cm path (a ~1.5 m loop), the loop is about 0.3 Ω before contact resistance, so the drop is
     about 0.8 V at stall.

   Fix: ask about 22 AWG wire. If the user has none, add a small spool, or run the servo's power pair through a
   cut 3-pin extension.
2. **Missing compatibility checks** (fields from interfaces.md):
   - **Arm power-off** (system budgets). This check is not in the file. The only e-stop plan is "unplugging the
     supply" (build_plan.md:18). When power is cut, the shoulder holds through belts and nothing self-locks.
     sim/v4_check.py gives a worst shoulder load of 2.459 N·m at the joint. XY42 detent torque is 150 g·cm
     (datasheet table), which is about 0.21 N·m at the joint through the 14:1, so the arm drops.
   - **Stepper cable to the HEM-17D2008-10.** compatibility.yaml:136-140 checks the cables against the XY42
     only. The HEM socket type and coil pinout are not recorded.
   - **XL4015 to the base posts.** There is no parts/models/xl4015-buck.yaml. base.py:33 places 2 holes at
     (±22.5, ±10), and the listing gave no hole data today.
   - **KW12-3 terminals to the jumpers.** The switch has solder lugs. The user did not confirm a soldering iron
     (they confirmed only items 5 and 6, the multimeter and hex keys).
   - **Fuse to wire** (current budget rule: "fuse ≤ wire rating"). There is no fuse and no confirmed wire gauge.
     See item 4.
   - **Motor shaft radial load from belt tension.** XY42 datasheet: "Max, radial force 28N (20mm from the
     flange)". Working belt force at 300 g is 1.175 N·m / 7 / 6.366 mm = 26.4 N on the elbow, and
     2.459 / 14 / 6.366 mm = 27.6 N on shoulder stage 1. Keeping the slack side taut needs about 13-14 N of
     pretension, which gives a shaft load of about 22-26 N. That passes, but only just. Record it.
3. **Base fixing has no part.** build_plan.md:28 says "two C-clamps: needed at full reach (not in the parts
   list)". compatibility.yaml:166-170 says overturning ≈ restoring moment. Clamps, or a board and screws, are

4. **E-stop and fuse are still open.** build_plan.md:18-19 has the e-stop `open` and the fuse `not bought`, and
