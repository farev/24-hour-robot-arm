## Blocking

1. **Fasteners (m3-m4-kit)**: nothing that is `owned` or `selected` supplies the screws and nuts. The only source is
   `m3-m4-kit`, and its status is `preferred` (components.yaml:251). The user's pick list (inbox/20261105-205046.md)
   does not include it. The v4 assembly takes 42 screws and 16 nuts from this kit: M3x6 ×4, M3x8 ×8, M3x10 ×8,
   M3x16 ×8, M3x20 ×4, M3x30 ×3, M4x30 ×2, M3 nuts ×11 and M4 nuts ×5 (counted from design/v4/assembly.yaml).
   design/v4/printed/base.py:94-98 also needs 6 M3 screws for the Uno (4) and buck (2) posts. These are not in the
   assembly or in the hardware note. The note at components.yaml:265 says "4 M4 nuts", but the assembly has 5.
   Fix: set the kit to `selected`, or record the user's own screw stock as `owned`. Fix the counts in the note.
2. **Wiring and connectors**: nothing that is owned or selected covers them, so the cart cannot wire up the arm.
   - **Motor cables (4 steppers in v4)**: there is no `owned` entry. `stepper-cable` is a `candidate`
     (components.yaml:337). The build plan says "buy only if missing" (build_plan.md:25), and nobody has checked.
     The cables also have to reach the elbow and wrist motors on the moving links. My estimate is about 0.6 m of
     cable path from the shield to the wrist motor with slack at the joints. Nothing checks this length.
   - **Servo lead**: `servo-extension` is `preferred`, not selected. The MG996R lead is 30 cm (TowerPro datasheet:
     "comes complete with 30cm wire"), and the note at components.yaml:317 gives the path as about 73 cm.
   - **Low-voltage wiring**: there is no hookup wire for 24 V to the buck input, for the buck output to the servo
     power (the plan splits the servo's red and brown wires to the buck and its signal to A3), or for the KW12-3
     to the D11 header. `dupont-jumpers` is only a `candidate`.
   Fix: confirm with the user which cables and wire they already own and add them as `owned`. Select the rest.

## Should fix

1. **Payload and reach do not meet requirements.yaml**. requirements.yaml asks for `payload_kg: 0.3` with
   `usable_torque_fraction: 0.5` and `reach_m: 0.30`. I ran `python sim/v4_check.py 0.3 0.26`: at 300 g the
   shoulder uses 0.675 and the elbow 0.646 of capacity, the most payload inside the 50 % budget is 0.129 kg, and
   the table reach is 287 mm. The drivers are set to 1.0 A on 1.2 A motors (compatibility.yaml:32,
   bring-up.md:22), so holding torque is about 0.26 × 1.0/1.2 = 0.217 N·m. With
   `python sim/v4_check.py 0.3 0.217` the fractions are 0.809 and 0.774, and the payload inside 50 % is
   **0.048 kg**. The user accepted about 150 g for v3 (decisions.md:33) and has not seen these numbers. Purchases
   do not change, but the user must decide. Fix: update requirements.yaml after the user agrees, or set 1.2 A with
   a fan.
2. **Budget over the "< $100" answer (questions/budget.yaml:10)**. Selected items come to $79.89. The fasteners
   and servo extension that are needed but only `preferred` add $24.69, and the glue adds $0.30, for $104.87.
   Third-party shipping read today adds $7.00: $4 for the 200 mm belts ("$4 delivery Monday, November 23") and
   $3 for the M4x70 ("$3 delivery Tuesday, November 24"). That gives $111.87 before tax, or about $121.8 at the
   8.9 % estimate. `orders[0].shipping_usd` is null and its note says this shipping was "not shown". Fix: ask the
   user to accept the overrun, or cut the servo-extension 10-pack (use a female-male jumper) and the KW12-3.
3. **Lead time**. Three third-party items arrive late: the 200 mm belts on Nov 23, the M4x70 on Nov 24, and the
   M3/M4 kit on Nov 17-27 ("FREE delivery November 17 - 27"). Everything else arrives Nov 10. These items hold
   back the shoulder stage 1 belt, the wrist belt, both shoulder axles and every screw. Fix: look for
   Amazon-shipped alternatives.
4. **Build plan and compatibility checks are still v2**.
   - build_plan.md:10-29 still lists the HEM on yaw, a spare XY42, a servo at the wrist, only 320 mm belts,
     140T/140T/120T pulleys and M4x20. v4 uses all four steppers (XY42 on yaw, shoulder and elbow, HEM on the
     wrist), two 200 mm belts and one servo.
   - compatibility.yaml:35-39 says to set 0.7 A "on the Z driver" for the HEM, but in v4 Z drives the XY42 yaw.
   - docs/bring-up.md:8-9 says "Yaw motor (HEM 34 mm, base) | Z socket" and "A socket | empty". The A socket now
     drives the 0.8 A HEM, and these docs give it no Vref value.
   - The belt check (compatibility.yaml:71-75) cites design/v2/belts.py.
   - The torque checks are for v2 (41 %) and v3 (150 g), not v4.
   - The supply budget counts 3 steppers and 2 servos.
5. **Missing compatibility checks** (fields from interfaces.md):
   - GT2 200 mm belt to pulleys and centres. I computed these from design/v4/geometry.py: shoulder stage 1
     (20T→72T) has a 51.3 mm centre distance and 142° wrap on the 20T; the wrist (20T→60T) has 58.6 mm and 155°.
     Both pass, but nothing records them.
   - The elbow 20T→140T on 320 mm has a 69.5 mm centre distance and 113° wrap, so only about 6.3 teeth are in
     mesh. That is at the usual 6-tooth minimum and should be recorded.
   - 20T pulley to the HEM shaft at the wrist. The existing check (compatibility.yaml:66-70) is about the v2
     yaw belt plane.
   - XY42 on the yaw socket: phase current.
   - Fasteners to printed holes and nut traps: no check at all.
   - MG996R signal to Uno A3; servo extension to the servo connector.
   - XL4015 to the base posts.
   - Stepper cable to the motor socket and the shield header, plus cable length.
   - v4 joint torque and the arm power-off behaviour.
6. **No spare F624ZZ**. v4 needs 10 (assembly.yaml), the pack holds exactly 10, and the listing says "Only 10 left
   in stock - order soon." Bearings go into PLA press fits after a fit-coupon test, so one damaged bearing stops
   the build. Fix: add the uxcell backup (B07Z3DB93B, $8.99, "Only 20 left", Nov 10). This conflicts with the
   budget in item 2.
7. **GT2 pulley listing and model disagree**. The listing B0DJQBZ6R1 says "15 mm wide and 15 mm high". The model
   is 16 × 16 with a ±0.5 tolerance (gt2-20t-pulley.yaml:12-13), and its belt-lane offset of 11.5 mm from the hub
   end is an estimate. The design hub gaps are 0.6 mm at the wrist and 1.4 mm at the elbow (geometry.py:76-78).
   The pulley slides on the shaft, so the lane can be set at assembly. Fix: measure on arrival and update the
   model.
8. **XL4015 has no model yaml**. parts/models/ has no `xl4015-buck.yaml`. The generator marks the holes
   "(+-22.5, +-10.0) [est; boards vary]" (xl4015-buck.py:3-4). The listing gives no hole data, and its size
   ("0.71"D x 0.91"W x 0.71"H") does not match the 51 × 26.3 mm modelled board. The base posts may not line up.
   `arduino-uno-r3` has no yaml either. Its hole positions in base.py:32 do match the Uno reference within 0.1 mm.
9. **Uno ↔ shield "verified" check lacks evidence for the pinout** (compatibility.yaml:40-44). The cited Allegro

10. **Base clamps are not in components**. build_plan.md:28 says "two C-clamps: needed at full reach", and

11. **Tools and assumed items are not in components.yaml**. These are not listed as owned:

      The XL4015 output range goes up to 36 V, so an unset buck can destroy the MG996R (rated 4.8-7.2 V).
    - A 2.5 mm hex key for M3. The M4x60 pack includes a 3 mm wrench.
    - A soldering iron for the KW12-3 terminals.
    - The USB-B cable (build_plan.md:27 "owned (assumed)").
    - PLA is `owned` but marked "assumed" (components.yaml:94). print_list.csv adds up to about 497 g.
12. **E-stop and fuse**.
    - build_plan.md:17 says to buy the inline switch for "Power switch / e-stop", but `dc-inline-switch` is a
      `candidate`.
    - Its current rating is "not on the listing".
    - docs/bring-up.md:14 wires the 24 V "through the inline switch".
    - The 24 V 6 A supply has no fuse, and the low-voltage wiring planned so far is Dupont jumpers.
    Fix: decide on the e-stop and update the docs. Name a wire gauge for the 24 V and 6 V runs, or add the 5 A fuse.

6. **Shield and supply electrical details.**
