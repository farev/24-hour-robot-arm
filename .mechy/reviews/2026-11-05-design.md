## Blocking

1. **Gripper pivots (grip-pivot, idler-pivot-a, idler-pivot-b, M3 x 30): the nut is only 1.0 mm on the thread.**
   Evidence: placed bounding boxes (home pose, same in every pose): `grip-pivot` y -25.0 .. 8.0 (tip at y 8.0),
   `grip-pivot-nut` y 7.0 .. 9.4; `idler-pivot-a` tip y 8.0, `idler-pivot-nut-a` y 7.0 .. 9.4 (same for b). So 1.0 of the
   2.4 mm nut (2 threads) is engaged, with the nut floating 0.2 mm above crank B / the idler link (`check`: clearance
   0.2 for all three nut pairs). Stack from the head: gripper base 5 + boss 17.9 (gripper_base.py:34, BOSS_H with
   FINGER_Y0 = 0.8) + 0.5 gap + link 5.4 + 0.2 + nut 2.4 + 0.5 pitch = 31.9 mm -> fdm_pla_rules §2 needs M3 x 35; the
   kit in components.yaml:254 is "6-30 mm". These pivots carry the gear-B and idler reaction of every grip; with two
   threads and "set just loose with thread glue" (summary.md:97) the nut will strip or back off and gear B / the links
   fall off. summary.md:91-93 says these nuts sit "in their nut traps"; neither crank_gear.py nor idler_link.py has a
   nut trap (inspect: only Ø3.2/3.4 holes).
   Fix (cheapest, no new screws): Ø6.5 x 3.3 counterbore (§2) on the gripper base bed face at the three pivots
   (gripper_base.py:62-63): the M3 x 30 tip moves to y 11.3, 1.9 mm past the nut, still clear of the jaws (they are not
   over the pivots). Or buy M3 x 35.

2. **MG996R horn height: the model puts the horn top 3.2 mm lower than the cited drawing; crank A, the A/B gear mesh and
   jaw A are built on it.** Evidence: mg996r-horn.step (inspect) z 2.3 .. 7.8 above the case top, i.e. 36.6 + 7.8 = 44.4
   mm over the case bottom. The datasheet the yaml cites (TowerPro MG996R PDF, drawing) gives 36.6 body and **47.6**
   overall with the horn fitted, i.e. horn top 11.0 above the case top. The horn is not in the yaml's critical_dims
   (mg996r-servo.yaml:20 calls it an estimate). Crank A sits on the horn top (geometry.py:97-98, FINGER_Y0 = 0.8); gear B
   and the idler links are positioned from the gripper base, not the horn. If the drawing is right, crank A rises 3.2
   mm: A teeth y 4.0 .. 10.0 against B teeth 1.4 .. 6.8 (face overlap 2.8 of 5.4 mm), and jaw A rests on the J1 boss at
   y 14.7 but on the J2 idler boss at 11.5, a 3.2 mm step over the 20 mm J1-J2 spacing, so jaw A tilts and its pins
   bind. Fix: measure the horn (top face above the case top, screw circle) before printing, add it to
   mg996r-servo.yaml with a source, and set GRIP_HORN_Y from it (raise gear B, idler links and bosses to match, or
   shim under crank A).

## Should fix

1. **Deck screws (deck-screw-1..4, M3 x 10) end inside their nuts.** Tip z 65.8, nut z 63.7 .. 66.1 (probe bounding
   boxes): 2.1 of 2.4 mm engaged, nothing protruding. §2: clamp 7.9 (pulley counterbore floor z 55.8 to the trap floor
   z 63.7) + nut 2.4 + 0.5 = 10.8 -> M3 x 12, which is what yaw_pulley.py:21 says ("M3 x 12 into the deck nut traps").
   The tip of an M3 x 12 is 0.3 mm above the deck top at (+-22.6, +-22.6), where no tower stands. Change the 4 parts
   to m3x12 and the kit count in components.yaml:259.
2. **Motor screws engage 3.0 to 4.0 mm, under §2's 1.5 x d = 4.5 mm.** Overlap with each motor's modelled 2.5 mm thread
   is 5.09 mm³ (yaw) and 6.79 mm³ (shoulder, elbow, wrist). Over the 1.70 mm² ring between the Ø2.9 shank and the
   Ø2.5 hole, that is 3.0 mm (yaw M3 x 6 under the 3 mm plate, yaw_motor_plate.py:5) and 4.0 mm (turret MOTOR_CB 3.0 in
   the 9 mm tower, shank MOTOR_CB 6.0 in the 10 mm plates). Fix: counterbores 0.5 mm deeper (turret.py:29, lower_shank.py:26,
   upper_shank.py:26) gives 4.5 mm with the same screws. For the yaw plate, measure the motor thread depth: use
   M3 x 8 if it is 5 mm or more, otherwise make the plate 3.5 mm.
3. **The base's bottom F624 inner ring bears on PLA under the yaw nut's preload.** base.py:74-80: the Ø13.1 pocket ends
   at z 13 in a ceiling with only the Ø4.8 bolt hole. The bearing (z 8 .. 13) has its inner-ring top face against that
   ceiling between r 2.4 and the inner-ring OD. The nut pulls the washer and inner ring up (base.py:18-20), and the inner
   ring turns with the bolt, so the clamp load goes through a steel-on-PLA sliding face instead of the balls. The yaw
   motor (6:1) sees that friction, and the PLA wears and creeps. Every other hub has a Ø7.6 relief between its
   bearings. Fix: open Ø4.8 to Ø7.6 from z 13 to z 41.5, which components.yaml:122 already describes for the non-flanged
   option.
4. **Gripper hardware is missing from the assembly and the BOM.**
   - The 4 servo tab screws (summary.md:99, M3 x 10 self-tapping) are not modelled. They engage 5 mm in the 5 mm plate,
     against §2's 2.5 x d = 7.5 mm for self-tapping.
   - The kit note (components.yaml:259) counts M3x10 x8 (4 deck + 4 shoulder), so these 4 are not counted.
   - The crank A horn screws (M2) and the thread glue (summary.md:97) are not in components.yaml.
5. **Crank A horn screw holes are undersized.** crank_gear.py:42-44 models Ø2.2 holes with a Ø3.8 x 2.0 counterbore.
   §2 for M2 is a 2.6 clearance hole with a 4.4 x 2.3 counterbore. Vertical holes print about 0.2 mm small, so the
   screw would also thread into crank A and could not clamp it to the horn.
6. **Printed gear mesh has no FDM allowance.** The gear centres are exactly 24.0 apart (pitch radius 12 + 12) and
   backlash is 0.15 (lib.py:68). The measured flank gap is 0.043 mm (home and open) and 0.126 mm (closed). §1 says outer
   features print 0.05 to 0.2 mm big, so the teeth may bind. summary.md:103 only says "check they mesh". Put the gear-B
   pivot at 24.3 mm, or add a gear pair to the fit coupon.
7. **The jaw "boss seat" recess is smaller than the bosses it should seat.** jaw.py:64 cuts Ø6.2 x 1.0, but the crank
   and idler pin bosses are Ø7.2 (crank_gear.py:35, idler_link.py:28, radius 3.6). The boss cannot enter, and the jaw
   bears on a 0.5 mm wide ring. Remove the recess or make it Ø7.8.
8. **allow_contact (137 pairs, no reason given for any, assembly.yaml:1533-1807).**
   - Whole motor against its mount, which the assembly skill says not to allow: yaw-motor/base,
     yaw-motor/yaw-motor-plate, shoulder-motor/turret, elbow-motor/lower-shank, wrist-motor/upper-shank.
   - Pairs that never touch in the home, open or closed pose: gripper-base/wrist-axle (3.05 mm), gripper-base/wrist-nut
     (1.12), axk2035/yaw-bolt (8.05), and the jaw/crank, jaw/idler, crank-B/base and idler/base pairs (0.5).
   - Measured: zero overlap in all of these, so nothing is hidden today, but they would hide future changes. Remove the
     pairs that do not touch and add a comment with the reason to the rest.
9. **Keep-outs and cable paths are missing.**
   - The yaw motor's plug has no keep-out. Placing the project's own xy42sth40-1204a-keepout.step on the yaw motor
     overlaps the base floor by 384 mm³ (x -122 .. -110, y +-8, z 2 .. 4). The socket bottom (z 4) sits on the floor
     top, so the plug and cable bend have no room. Fix: extend the floor cut-out under the connector to the base edge
     and add the keep-out.
   - The HEM wrist motor's rear connector has no keep-out model.
   - The gripper servo lead and the wrist and elbow cables have no modelled route across the +-150 deg wrist and yaw
     travel.
10. **PLA in contact with stepper faces (§8).** The XY42STH datasheet gives "Temperature Rise 80 °C Max. (rated current)".
    At 1.0 of 1.2 A that is about 0.69 x 80 = 55 °C of rise, so a case near 78 °C in a 23 °C room. That is above PLA's
    55 to 60 °C. Affected: the turret's right tower (holds the shoulder motor against belt tension), the lower and upper
    shanks, and the yaw plate. compatibility.yaml:143 only says to check the temperature. Print these 4 parts in PETG,
    or measure the case temperature in a held pose before trusting PLA.
11. **PLA race washers (x8) and spacer tubes in clamped M4 stacks (§8, constant clamp: PLA creeps and the preload drops).**
    Use steel M4 washers. summary.md:84-85 already names DIN 125 plus a shim as the fallback.
12. **Uno and buck posts: the pilots are 5 mm deep (base.py:97-98), but the BOM says M3 x 8-10.** Through the 1.6 mm PCB,
    an M3 x 8 needs 6.4 mm and an M3 x 10 needs 8.4 mm, so the screws bottom out in solid floor. Run the Ø2.6 pilot down
    through the floor (9 mm), or specify M3 x 6.
13. **Elephant's foot and the fit coupon.** No part has the style's C_BED 0.4 chamfer (style.py:11). The F624 pockets
    of the wrist pulley, both shanks and the shoulder compound, and the base's Ø15.4 bore, all open on the bed face.
    The coupon cuts its pockets from the top face (fit_coupon.py:29-33), so it does not test the bed-face press fit the
    parts use. The turret's horizontal M4 holes are Ø4.2 (turret.py:93), and §1 says +0.1 for horizontal holes. They
    are also not on the coupon.
14. **Print list and records.**
    - fit-coupon.stl exists and summary.md:86 says to print it first, but it has no row in print_list.csv.
    - summary.md has wrong statements:
      - "J2 nuts sit inside the idler links' nut traps" (summary.md:93): measured, the nut bottom face rests on the
        link face (0.0009 / 0.0012 mm³ overlap).
      - "~6 mm from the slots beside the beam to the lower shank's first beam window" (summary.md:64): computed from
        lower_shank.py:47-57 it is 4.6 mm, which still meets §4.
    - The "-1.7" heights in the docstrings at geometry.py:94-98, crank_gear.py:6 and gripper_base.py are stale; the
      code uses FINGER_Y0 = 0.8.
15. **Looks and legibility (legible_cad §5).**
    - Printed idler links are coloured #222222 (the servo colour) and the jaws #E8A33D. Neither is in style.py COLORS
      or named as a filament in the summary.
    - The print list names `idler-link-a` (qty 2) and `yaw-race-washer` (qty 8, used at five joints). These do not
      match the file stems idler_link and race_washer.
    - Mirrored pairs end in -a/-b, not -left/-right.
    - Literal radii appear in printed sources (gripper_base.py:45 and :54, idler and jaw outlines). No touched edge is
      rounded (jaw pads).
    - The 13 x 11 mm disc windows (both shanks) are open 11 mm holes, inside the 10.2-35.6 mm finger-trap band. The
      lower shank's windows turn 1 mm from the left tower's inner face.

## Unverified

1. MG996R tab holes 49.5 x 10 and the shaft offset of 10 are estimates, and the gripper base holes are placed from them.
   The drawing's "26.6" does not clearly show whether it runs to the tab underside or the tab top, which moves the
   servo 2.5 mm. Confirm with calipers (the caliper is a candidate in components.yaml).
2. The MG996R horn screw circle (7 mm, crank_gear.py:15) is an estimate. Measure the horn.
3. The XL4015 board has no parts/models yaml. The base posts use the model's two diagonal holes (+-22.5, +-10, Ø3.0)
   with no source. Read the listing (B0D1G4C28P) or measure the board.
4. arduino-uno-r3, the fastener models and the horn have no yaml. The Uno post positions (14.0/2.5, 15.3/50.7,
   66.1/7.6, 66.1/35.5) match the Arduino Uno hole pattern I know, but the shield stack height (model z 45) has no
   source. The docs.arduino.cc page gives no dimensions, only a CAD zip.
5. HEM-17D2008-10: all dimensions except the 34 mm length are NEMA 17 estimates (no datasheet). XY42STH: the datasheet
   has no drawing and no -1204A row, so the M3 thread depth (4.5) and the connector position are estimates. Both
   affect Should fix 2 and 9.
6. Yaw travel between the checked angles: sim/yaw_sweep.py was not run. I checked +150 (pose file) and -150 (yaw-limit
   rotated by -300 deg in memory): no interference.
