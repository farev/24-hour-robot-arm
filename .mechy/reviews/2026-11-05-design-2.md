## Blocking

1. **Wrist joint limit (q3 = +-150) drives the gripper into the upper shank and wrist motor; the real free range is about +-100.**
   Evidence: geometry.py:20 `Q3_LIM = (-150.0, 150.0)`, firmware/arm_v4/arm_v4.ino:39 `Q3_MIN = -150, Q3_MAX = 150`, and
   `jointsOk` (arm_v4.ino:84-88) checks nothing else. Sweep at th1 90, th2 0 (check_assembly, overlap volume):
   q3 +-90: 0; +-100: 2.7 mm3 (upper-shank/idler-link-b); +-105: 154 mm3; +-120: 1074 mm3; +-135: 2551 mm3;
   +-150: wrist-motor/jaw 3035 mm3, upper-shank/crank-gear-a 1609 mm3, wrist-motor-pulley/crank-gear-a 468 mm3.
   The crank gears, idler links and jaws (world y 0.8..18) share the upper shank's y band (0..10) and the wrist motor's
   (10..44). This is reached by ordinary commands, not only by J at the limit: th1 60, th2 20, wr -90 (gripper down,
   a `P` target around x 226, z 208) gives q3 = -110 and 6 interferences, max 303 mm3 (upper-shank/crank-gear-a).
   None of the 7 pose files has |q3| > 90. Fix: set Q3 to about -95..+95 in geometry.py and the firmware (and make `ik`
   reject targets that need more), or move the gripper linkage out of the upper shank's plane; add pose files at the new
   wrist limits.

2. **Elbow/shoulder limit combinations put the arm into the turret, base and yaw motor; no pose file sits at th1 = 140 or
   q2 = +150.** Evidence (check_assembly on in-memory poses, q3 = 0 unless noted):
   - th1 140, q2 +120: 19 interferences, wrist-motor/shoulder-motor 6306 mm3; q2 +135: 55, max 14404 mm3; q2 +150: 80
     (wrist motor 11009 mm3 into the turret, gripper into the AXK and base).
   - th1 90, q2 +150: 19 interferences, max 1511 mm3 (gripper into the shoulder compound and I bearing).
   - th1 20, q2 -135 (the folded limit at low shoulder): gripper into the base, 3375 mm3.
   th1 140 with q2 up to +100 and th1 90 with q2 up to +130 are clean. Firmware limits (arm_v4.ino:38-39: TH1 20..140,
   Q2 -135..150) are per joint only, so `J` accepts all of these. geometry.py:18-19 says the -135 limit came from a "CAD
   pose check", but only q2 = -135 at th1 110 with the wrist tilted was posed (elbow-folded).
   Fix: lower Q2_MAX (about +100 at th1 140) or add a combined check in `jointsOk` (wrist point above the base and
   outside the turret envelope), then add pose files at th1 140, q2 max and q2 min with th1 20.

3. **Gripper servo horn height: the model is 3.2 mm lower than the cited drawing, and crank A, the gear mesh and jaw A are
   built on it.** Evidence: mg996r-horn.step (inspect) z 2.3..7.8 above the case top; the drawing in the cited PDF
   shows 36.6 body and 47.6 overall with the horn, i.e. horn top 11.0 above the case top. geometry.py:97-98 seats crank A
   on the horn (FINGER_Y0 = 0.8), while crank B, the idler links and the base bosses are placed from the gripper base
   (gripper_base.py:33-34). With the drawing value crank A rises 3.2 mm: A teeth y 4.0..10.0 against B 1.4..6.8 and jaw A
   tilts across its 20 mm pin spacing. The horn is not in mg996r-servo.yaml critical_dims. summary.md:118-123 already
   says not to print the gripper parts; until the horn is measured and the gripper regenerated, gripper_base,
   crank_gear_a/b, idler_link and jaw_a/b do not fit as modelled.

## Should fix

1. **I-axle nut spins in its slot.** turret.py:105-108 cuts the nut slot at radius `M4_NUT[0]/sqrt(3) + 0.1` = 4.29
   (8.57 wide); an M4 nut's corners are 8.08 max (radius 4.04), so the nut turns with the bolt, recessed 3.5 mm where
   no wrench fits. This is also the stage-2 tension adjustment, so it is loosened and re-tightened. Fix: slot width =
   across flats + 0.3 = 7.3 (§2 side-entry slot) so the flats bear on the slot sides while the nut still slides.
2. **Right tower overhangs the two +y deck nut traps by 0.5 mm.** turret.step material directly above the trap
   openings (z 5..15): 6.95 mm3 at (22.6, 22.6) and 18.14 mm3 at (-22.6, 22.6), both y 25.0..25.5. A real M3 nut
   (AF 5.32..5.5) has its flat at y 25.29..25.38, so it cannot drop in straight. overhang_faces.py lists the same two
   roofs at z 5 (0.6 and 1.8 mm2). Fix: move the screw circle (turret.py:111, yaw_pulley.py:21) to r 31 or rotate it
   off the tower band.
3. **Overhangs with a wrong or no reason.** shoulder_compound.py:29-30: the flange cone under the 36T goes from r 10.5 to
   r 12.2 in 1.0 mm (59.6 deg from vertical); summary.md:78 counts the "compound taper" as 45 deg exactly (true only for
   the 72T taper). The turret's slots along u_i and u_m are inclined 20 deg, so their roofs are 70 deg overhangs about
   8 mm long (overhang_faces.py: z 56.5 and 58.6, 103.5 mm2 each, pilot slot; z 34-37, I-axle slots), not the "8-9 mm
   bridges" in summary.md:76. Make the cone 45 deg (1.7 mm tall) and either accept and state the sloped roofs or give
   the slots a flat or 45 deg roof in print Z.
4. **Running joints rely on free nuts and thread glue that is not in the BOM.** The gear-B and idler pivot nuts sit
   0.2 mm above the parts (check: 0.2 mm in every pose); the J1/J2 nuts clamp jaw and link together if tightened, so
   they also have to stay loose (summary.md:126-127). No threadlocker or nyloc nut is in components.yaml. J1-A: M3 x 20
   tip at y -2.0, nut y -1.6..0.8, 0.4 mm past the nut (§2 asks for 1 pitch, 0.5). Add M3 nyloc nuts (or threadlocker)
   to the BOM.
5. **Crank A horn screws.** crank_gear.py:44-45 counterbore Ø3.8 x 2.0; §2 for M2 is 4.4 x 2.3 (an M2 SHCS head is
   3.8 x 2.0, so no clearance, and the hole prints about 0.2 small). The horn screws (M2 or the servo's own) are not a
   BOM item (components.yaml:259 only mentions them).
6. **Gripper servo window below the §1 clearance.** gripper_base.py:24 CLR 0.4 per side gives 20.5 x 41.5. §1 for a
   bought body: body max + 2 x (tol + 0.3) = 19.7 + 1.2 = 20.9 and 40.7 + 1.2 = 41.9 (tol 0.3, mg996r-servo.yaml:9-10).
   The drawing's end view is labelled 20, not 19.7.
7. **Servo tab screws.** Not in the assembly. M3 x 10 self-tapping through the 2.5 mm tab into the 5 mm plate engages
   5 mm; §2 asks 2.5 x d = 7.5 mm for self-tapping. Add bosses under the tabs or use M3 x 12 with nuts.
8. **Yaw motor screws engage 3 mm** (overlap 5.089 mm3 / 1.696 mm2 ring = 3.0 mm; yaw_motor_plate.py:5 M3 x 6 under
   a 3 mm plate), below §2's 1.5 x d = 4.5 mm. Use M3 x 8 if the motor's thread is 5 mm or deeper, or a 3.5 mm plate.
9. **Motor pulley set screws are buried in the mounts.** Set screws at hub z 3.5 sit at world y -11.1 inside the lower
   shank plate (y -8..-18, elbow), y 3.9 inside the upper shank (0..10, wrist) and y 28 inside the right tower (25..34,
   shoulder). They can be tightened only before the motor is mounted, the hub gaps (1.4, 0.6, 0.5 mm) cannot be checked
   in place, and a loose pulley means taking the motor off. Write the assembly order, or give the pulley room outside
   the plate.
10. **Wrist 60T has no belt flange on the +y side.** wrist_pulley.py:20-22: flange (hub r 21) only on the gripper side;
    the teeth (y -7.2..-1.0) face the upper shank, whose outline near W is the r 13 circle, inside the 18.8 mm tooth
    radius. Nothing stops the belt walking off toward +y except the motor pulley's flanges.
11. **PLA where §8 says no.** The turret's right tower, both shanks and the yaw plate touch stepper faces, and the 8 PLA
    race washers and the spacer tubes carry the axle clamp loads. assumptions/motor-mount-material.yaml records the
    user's choice (PLA plus a 10-minute temperature check), so this stays open until that check is done.
12. **Keep-outs and cable paths.** No keep-out for the HEM wrist motor's rear connector (hem-17d2008-10.yaml:23-24),
    the CNC shield's motor and power headers, or the servo lead. The shield stack top is z 54 and the turret tails and
    shoulder motor sweep over the board at z 62.5 and up, which leaves 8.5 mm for plugs and cable bends there. There is
    no route for the shoulder, elbow, wrist and servo cables across the +-150 deg yaw and the wrist. There are no
    hex-key access keep-outs.
13. **allow_contact.**
    - The reasons exist only in build_assembly.py (`ok(a, b, why)`, line 71). build_assembly.py:292 drops them, so
      assembly.yaml:1546-1822 has 137 pairs with no reason.
    - Pairs that never touch in any of the 8 poses (measured): yaw-bolt/axk2035 8.05 mm, wrist-axle/gripper-base 3.05 mm.
    - Whole-part pairs: yaw-motor/base, yaw-motor/yaw-motor-plate, shoulder-motor/turret, elbow-motor/lower-shank,
      wrist-motor/upper-shank, jaw/crank, jaw/idler, idler/gripper-base, crank-gear-b/gripper-base, gripper-base/wrist-pulley.
    - Measured in all 8 poses: 0 overlap for these, except numerical noise of 0.02-0.03 mm3 at yaw 150. So nothing is
      hidden today, but they will hide future changes. Drop the non-touching pairs and write the reasons into the yaml.
14. **Records and looks.**
    - Nut models are undersized: fasteners.py:30 uses e_min/2 + 0.05 as the circumradius, giving AF 5.29 (ISO M3 5.5)
      and AF 6.72 (M4 7.0), so nut-in-trap checks are optimistic by about 0.2 mm.
    - No printed part imports style.py: no C_BED 0.4 bed chamfer, no R_EDGE on touched edges (jaw pads).
    - The idler links (#222222, the servo colour) and the jaws (#E8A33D) are not in COLORS or listed as filaments.
    - The 11 mm disc windows on both shanks are inside the 10.2-35.6 mm finger-trap band, and the lower shank's windows
      sweep past the left tower's edge.
    - summary.md:78 (cone angles) and the "0 interferences" statements hold only for the 8 posed angles; see Blocking 1-2.
