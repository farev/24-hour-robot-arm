# v4 arm: screws, nuts and washers

Counted from design/v4/assembly.yaml (every modelled fastener) plus the ones the assembly doesn't model (Uno and buck posts, servo tabs). SHCS = socket head cap screw. All of them come from three Amazon packs in the cart (docs/amazon-cart.md): an M3 kit (6-30 mm + nuts), an M4 kit (6-30 mm + nuts) and M4 x 70 (also the yaw axle, on a printed 10 mm spacer, so no M4 x 60 is needed). The "From" column gives how many the pack holds.

**66 fasteners in all**, of which 22 are for the gripper (needed only when the gripper is built, after the servo measurement).

## Not in the screw packs

- **Servo horn screws (4):** use the small self-tapping screws from the MG996R box to fix crank gear A to the horn. If the box has none: M2 x 6-8 self-tapping (crank A holes are 2.6 mm clearance).
- **Pulley set screws:** come with the GT2 20T pulleys (2 per pulley, M3 grub screws; 2.5 mm or 1.5 mm hex key depending on the pulley).

## Washers (printed, on the arm plates)

- 7 x race washer 1.0 mm: yaw (1), shoulder axle (2), intermediate axle (2), elbow (2).
- 1 x wrist washer 1.7 mm: wrist axle, between the upper shank and the wrist pulley.
- 1 x yaw bolt spacer (8 x 10 mm tube): under the yaw axle M4 x 70 head, inside the turret deck hole.
- No steel washers are needed.

## Thread locking

The gripper pivot and jaw-pin nuts must stay just loose so the parts turn: use a drop of medium threadlocker or nyloc nuts (7 x M3; the kits have plain nuts only).
