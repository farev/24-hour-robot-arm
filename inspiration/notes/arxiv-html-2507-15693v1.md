- Mechanism and layout: Serial 3-DOF+ arm with printed PLA links; belt + capstan stages at base and shoulder.
- Actuators, transmission, bearings, specs (cite timestamps/frames): J2 NEMA23 belt 2.5:1 x capstan = 18.75:1; base NEMA17 capstan 8:1; Uno + A4988/TB6600 at 24 V; 0.63 kg measured at 467 mm reach, repeatability SD 0.29-0.59 mm, $212 (paper).
